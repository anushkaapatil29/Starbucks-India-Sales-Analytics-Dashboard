# Starbucks India – Sales & Business Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze Starbucks India's sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and store-level business metrics.

The dashboard provides business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, geographical analysis, and customer segmentation.

## Dashboard Pages

1. **Overview:** Overall sales, total orders, average order value, customers, and stores.
2. **Sales Analysis:** Monthly sales trends, regional performance, profitability, and customer-type analysis.
3. **Store Analysis:** Store-wise revenue, city performance, store formats, and profitability.
4. **Product Analysis:** Top-selling products, category revenue, profit margins, and product performance.
5. **Customer Analysis:** Customer segmentation, monthly ranking, acquisition trends, and customer ratings.
6. **Business Report:** Revenue performance, key metrics, and executive takeaways.

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Sales: ₹43,652.50
- Total Orders: 100
- Average Order Value: ₹436.53
- Total Customers: 100
- Total Stores: 10
- Total Units Sold: 173
- Overall Profit Margin: 5.6%

## Key Business Insights

- South region generated approximately ₹19,038 in revenue.
- West region contributed approximately ₹18,722.50 in revenue.
- Hot Coffee and Cold Coffee are leading product categories.
- Mumbai recorded the highest city-level revenue.
- Latte emerged as a leading revenue-generating product.
- Overall profit margin stood at 5.6%.
